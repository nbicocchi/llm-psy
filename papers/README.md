# Reference papers

Local PDF copies are excluded from Git by default. Preserve personal research copies locally and link to publisher or preprint records rather than assuming redistribution rights.

- Li et al. (2022), *Is GPT-3 a Psychopath? Evaluating Large Language Models from a Psychological Perspective*. [arXiv:2212.10529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10529). The project used the v1 appendices for SD-3 and BFI transcription; its protocol is not an exact replication.
- Hagendorff et al. (2026), *Machine psychology for language models*. [Science Advances, DOI:10.1126/sciadv.aec1912](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aec1912).

Additional candidate literature is documented in `ideas/relational-feedback-histories.md`. Research proposals do not establish that a method is novel or validated.
