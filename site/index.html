Three views of the same saved observations

Heatmap for an overview · Repetition plot for one scale · Scatter plot for two scales.

Heatmap colors show position in each scale's theoretical range (0–100), not human percentiles or a common psychological unit. Higher is not necessarily better. Gray cells are missing scores, never neutral answers.

Runtime and hardware groups stay separate. Different questionnaire or generation protocols have separate columns; model artifacts and thinking modes have separate rows. Comparing different modes is descriptive, not a controlled estimate of a family or size effect.
