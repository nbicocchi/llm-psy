More score series do not mean more independent evidence: instruments share constructs and sometimes identical items. Scores describe responses, not verified personality, honesty, competence or safety.

SD-3 and BFI use item means (1–5). SWLS uses a sum (5–35), FS a sum (8–56), and the IPIP scales use sums (10–50 per trait). Do not compare raw scores across different ranges or pool overlapping scales.
