Historical visual stimuli · Separate exploratory experiment

Ten historical plates.

Digital reproductions of Hermann Rorschach's 1921 inkblots, archived from Wikimedia Commons. These are not certified clinical stimulus materials.

Before viewing

Exposure to these plates may affect a future human assessment. This laboratory does not administer a standardized clinical Rorschach test and assigns no personality or diagnostic scores to image descriptions.

These widely published images and their familiar interpretations may be present in model training data. Responses cannot establish whether a model is interpreting an image or recalling learned associations.

The historical plates form rorschach-exploratory-v1. Browse the visual stimulus library and open a plate to compare saved model descriptions. Synthetic illustrations are listed separately from these historical reproductions.
